How a TPI screening helps your golf

Your body and your swing are connected. A TPI screening looks at how mobility, stability, balance and strength may influence your swing - identifying physical restrictions and compensations, then turning the findings into clear training priorities.

1

Move more freely

Improve usable movement through the hips, upper back, shoulders and ankles, making it easier to rotate and reach good positions.

2

Posture & balance

Build the core, pelvic control and single-leg stability needed to stay centred and control your body through the swing.

3

Reduce compensations

When the body can't make a movement it finds another way. Addressing restrictions makes the swing feel less forced and more repeatable.

4

Speed & power

Improve strength, sequencing and force transfer so you create speed efficiently rather than relying on effort alone.

5

Train around limitations

Appropriate mobility, strength and stability work while respecting injury history, pain and individual movement capacity.

6

A personalised plan

Focus on the exercises that matter most to your screen, instead of a general golf programme that may not match your needs.

Physical limitations may contribute to:

  • Sway or slide
  • Early extension
  • Loss of posture
  • Reverse spine angle
  • Over-the-top pattern
  • Limited turn or follow-through
